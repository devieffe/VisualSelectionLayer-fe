Who is responsible?

Dev Ieffe operates this website. For privacy questions or requests, contact webmail@ieffe.dev.

The core website

The selection engine runs in your browser. This page does not send your selected text to us. Fonts and demo images are hosted with the site, rather than requested from Google Fonts.

The published site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Its infrastructure receives connection information, such as your IP address and request information, to deliver the website and protect its service. See the GitHub privacy statement for its processing and retention practices. Website delivery and security are based on legitimate interests under GDPR Article 6(1)(f).

Your privacy preference

We use browser local storage under the key vsl-cookie-choice to remember your choices. The record contains separate CodePen and UserWay preferences, the notice version and the time of your choice. It is stored on your device, not sent to our server.

The preference is used for up to 180 days, after which optional services remain blocked until you choose again. An expired record may remain in browser storage until replaced or cleared. Remembering your privacy choice is necessary to respect your decision; it is not used for tracking.

Optional services: off until you agree

CodePen live preview: enabling this connects to CodePen and its asset/preview domains to display an interactive demo. These providers receive connection and device information, including your IP address, and may use cookies or other browser storage. See CodePen's privacy policy for its purposes and retention practices.

UserWay accessibility widget: enabling this loads accessibility controls from UserWay. UserWay receives connection and device information and may use cookies or browser storage for its service. See UserWay's privacy policy for its purposes and retention practices. The site remains usable without this optional widget.

Our activation of these optional integrations is based on your consent under GDPR Article 6(1)(a). They may involve processing outside the EEA. Their linked notices describe provider processing and transfer arrangements; this site does not control their retention periods.

Changing or withdrawing your choice

Open consent settings to enable services separately or select Reject all / withdraw consent. The Consent settings button is always available on the main page. The preview's enable button enables only CodePen, not UserWay. Rejecting optional services does not prevent access to the main page or local demo images.

Withdrawing permission for a running service reloads the page, with that integration blocked, to stop its code. Withdrawal stops future optional loading; it cannot undo data already transmitted or delete storage on another provider's domain. You can clear that storage in your browser settings. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.
